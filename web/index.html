Run details and limitations


What this builds
Genomic baseline
Measured binary genomic indicators only.
Full comparison
The same genomic indicators plus linked clinical and geographic features.
Before uploading
The outcome must be a laboratory-derived resistance label. Unknown and intermediate labels are not silently treated as susceptible.
Both susceptible and resistant examples are required. A technically successful run does not establish clinical usefulness.
No synthetic extensions
Do not invent ages, exposure histories, or locations to extend a genomic dataset. Population summaries cannot be joined to unrelated individual genomes as patient observations.
Training teaches the model.
Prediction applies it.
New predictions do not retrain it.

Reading the output
Prediction
The class assigned using the fixed training-run threshold.
Resistance score
An uncalibrated Random Forest score from 0 to 1. It is not a validated personal risk estimate.
Input warnings
Missing values, unseen categories, or values outside the observed training range.
"Predicted susceptible" is not an antibiotic recommendation and does not establish that a treatment is safe or effective.
